# NyxWatch — Design & Visual Reference

## Color Palette (Strict 70 / 20 / 10 Ratio)
- **70% Jet Black** (`#0B0B0C`) — primary canvas, backgrounds, ticker bar, deep space
- **20% Smoke Grey & Charcoal** (`#9A9D9E` / `#141416`) — cards, typography, borders, hardware chassis
- **10% Cherry Red** (`#DC1D2E`) — surgical accents only: `BUSTED` circular stamp, `ALERT` badge, active shield, primary CTA
- **Crisp White** (`#FFFFFF`) — primary headlines & telemetry text

## Capture Overlay Standard (`CameraCapture.applyBustedStamp`)
- **Top-Right**: Circular double-ring Cherry Red (`#DC1D2E`) stamp tilted `-12°` with centered `BUSTED` + `NYXWATCH`.
- **Bottom**: Full-width Jet Black (`#0B0B0C`) teleprompter news ticker with a `2px` Cherry Red top border, left `ALERT` badge, and auto-fitted monospaced telemetry (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm:ss • GPS lat, lon • NET en0: IP • NYXWATCH`).
